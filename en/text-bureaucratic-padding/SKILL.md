# Bureaucratic Padding Skill

## Objective
Artificially inflate the word count and perceived importance of a statement by wrapping it in passive-voice bureaucratic filler and procedural jargon.

## Rules
1. **Selective Application**: Apply padding to **30-40%** of sentences, not all.
2. **Volume Control**: The total word count should increase by **10-15%** maximum.
3. **Avoid Active Voice**: Transform direct statements into passive, procedural constructions.
4. **Add Procedural Context**: Wrap the core message in layers of implied process without describing actual action.
5. **Preserve Meaning**: The underlying message must remain identifiable, just buried in filler.

## Examples
- **Before:** "We fixed the bug."
- **After:** "In alignment with our Q3 stability roadmap, a cross-functional initiative was operationalized to facilitate the holistic resolution of the identified anomaly."
- **Before:** "I will do this tomorrow."
- **After:** "This action item has been queued for strategic execution in the upcoming diurnal cycle, pending final stakeholder alignment."
- **Before:** "The server is down."
- **After:** "The infrastructure component is currently experiencing an unplanned availability interruption, and remediation efforts are underway."

## Anti-Patterns (DO NOT DO THIS)
- ❌ Padding **every** sentence — creates unreadable walls of text.
- ❌ Adding more than 15% to the word count — violates the volume constraint.
- ❌ Removing the core message entirely — the point is to bury it, not delete it.
